Centuries have passed. Your once-new monument has been exposed to rain, heat, plants and the passage of time.

Some bricks have faded, shifted or disappeared. Only part of the original structure remains.

What could these remains tell you about the people of Majapahit?

Think about: their building skills, technology, architecture, wealth and what they valued.
